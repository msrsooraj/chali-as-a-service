About

Chali as a Service is a small, independently run website that serves up random jokes and memes — just for laughs, nothing more.

There are no accounts, no tracking, and no data collection on our end. The site is supported by ads.

API

Want chali in your own app? We have a simple API:

GET /api

Returns a random joke as JSON.

The ads? Yeah, servers don't run on good vibes — cash venam. Vere vazhi illa..

Contribute

Got a good chali? Drop it on GitHub.

Add your joke to chali.txt and separate it from others with a --- on its own line. Multiline jokes are supported!
