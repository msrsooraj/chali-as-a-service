Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This website does not collect any personal information directly. There are no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no email collection.

Tools (QR Code Generator, etc.)

This site has no database. Any text you enter into a tool (like the QR code generator) is processed in memory to produce your result and is never saved, logged, or stored anywhere — not on disk, not in a database, not in analytics. Once your result is generated, the input is gone.

Third-Party Advertising

This site uses third-party advertising services to display ads. These advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing activity on this site and other websites.

These cookies allow advertising partners to:

  • Show ads that may be relevant to your interests
  • Limit how often you see a particular ad
  • Measure the effectiveness of ads

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. However, third-party advertising services may place cookies on your device. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect how some ads are displayed.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Information Collected by Third Parties

Third-party vendors may collect certain data (such as your IP address, browser type, and browsing behaviour) through cookies and similar technologies. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the privacy practices of third-party services.

Your Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area or another region with data protection laws, you have the right to control how your data is used by third-party advertisers. You can exercise this by adjusting your cookie and ad personalisation settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
